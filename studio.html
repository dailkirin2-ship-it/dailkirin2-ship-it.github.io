About

STUDIO

sran.dev is the studio handle of Danielle Choster — an indie developer based in Russia building games, apps, and digital projects on Unity with an attitude.

Started as Bold & Easy — a Telegram ecosystem project. Then came the app. Then the games. Now it's a full indie studio under the sran.dev domain.

The stack is Unity, the attitude is indie, and the output is real. No corporate nonsense, no fake portfolios — just shipped projects and honest work.
